![Lockdown Mode](/images/zen_os/lockdown_mode.webp)

Lockdown Mode locks things down so you (or a kid, or a guest) can just read. It turns on Zen Mode and keeps it on until Lockdown is off. KOReader asks to restart when you toggle it.

Toggle it with the **Lockdown** button in Controls or the `ZenOS: Toggle Lockdown Mode` action.

## What you can lock

**Library**
- **Disable context menu**: no holding books to edit, delete, or move.

**Controls**
- **Require hold to toggle buttons**: stops accidental taps.
- **Disable settings panel**: hides the way into settings.

**Reader**
- **Disable bottom menu swipe**: no page browser or reader menu.
- **Disable multi-word selection**
- **Disable word search on hold**

**Magnify UI** switches the Library to a 2×2 grid and 3-item list for bigger targets, then puts your old layout back when Lockdown turns off.

**Where:** Zen Settings > Extras > Lockdown mode
